Mot mau nen khong co y nghia neu tach khoi boi canh

Pin bar, engulfing, inside bar hay doji deu chi la dau vet. Viec cua trader la doc dau vet do trong dung cau chuyen thi truong.

O dau? Gan ho tro, khang cu, trendline, dinh cu, day cu hay o giua vung nhieu?
Sau dieu gi? Sau trend manh, pullback yeu, breakout, false break hay climax?
Co tiep dien khong? Nen sau co follow-through hay gia dung lai va dao chieu?
Cau hoi dung khong phai la "nen nay ten gi?" ma la "ai dang bi ket va ai dang kiem soat?"
